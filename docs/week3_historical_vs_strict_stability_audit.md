# Week 3 — Historical vs strict gene-stability audit

## Objective

This audit compares the historical stable-gene output from the original
GSE160638 modelling workflow with the stability results obtained from the
strict nested cross-validation rebuild.

The purpose is reproducibility assessment, not retrospective optimization of
the historical signature.

## Historical reference

The historical 5/5 stable-gene set was obtained from:

`results/stable_genes_5of5_final_100.csv`

The historical workflow identified:

- 13 genes selected in 5/5 folds.

The historical workflow also contained full-dataset Random Forest variable
importance analyses. Those importance values are considered exploratory and
are not used to construct the strict consensus candidates.

## Strict nested-CV stability

The rebuilt nested-CV procedure performs feature selection exclusively inside
training partitions.

Observed strict stability:

- selected in at least 1/5 outer folds: 312 genes;
- selected in at least 2/5 outer folds: 100 genes;
- selected in at least 3/5 outer folds: 51 genes;
- selected in at least 4/5 outer folds: 25 genes;
- selected in 5/5 outer folds: 12 genes.

The 12 genes selected in all five outer folds are currently treated as
**core consensus candidates**.

They do not constitute an independently validated final signature.

## Historical-versus-strict comparison

Historical 5/5 genes: 13

Strict 5/5 genes: 12

Shared 5/5 genes: 0

Historical-only genes: 13

Strict-only genes: 12

Therefore, the historical and strict 5/5 stability sets have zero overlap.

## Historical genes inside the strict rebuild

All 13 historical genes are present in the validated 17,002-gene Week 1 input
matrix.

However:

- historical genes present in validated input: 13/13;
- historical genes selected in at least 1/5 strict outer folds: 0/13;
- historical genes selected in at least 2/5 strict outer folds: 0/13;
- historical genes selected in at least 3/5 strict outer folds: 0/13;
- historical genes selected in at least 4/5 strict outer folds: 0/13;
- historical genes selected in 5/5 strict outer folds: 0/13.

Thus, the absence of overlap cannot be explained by absence of the historical
genes from the validated input feature space.

The historical stable set is not reproduced by the strict nested-CV feature
selection procedure.

## Interpretation boundary

This result does **not** demonstrate that the historical genes are biologically
irrelevant.

It demonstrates that their previously reported selection stability is not
reproduced under the stricter nested-CV architecture implemented in the
rebuild.

Likewise, the 12 strict 5/5 genes must not be described as an independently
validated final molecular signature.

The independently validated performance estimates remain those calculated from
pooled outer out-of-fold predictions generated by the strict nested-CV
pipeline.

Consensus-gene analyses performed after nested CV are stability and candidate
signature analyses and must remain separate from validation-performance claims.

## Reproducibility

The audit is reproduced from the repository root with:

```bash
Rscript --vanilla scripts/08_compare_historical_strict_stability.R
```

Primary outputs:

- `results/week3_historical_vs_strict_5of5.csv`
- `results/week3_historical_vs_strict_5of5_summary.csv`
- `results/week3_historical_13_genes_in_strict_pipeline.csv`
- `results/week3_historical_13_genes_in_strict_pipeline_summary.csv`
